FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

How long does a website take?

Most sites are live in one to three weeks, depending on the package and how quickly I get your photos and details.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work, and a short call about your business. I help with the wording.

Are hosting and a domain included?

They are billed separately from the package. I set them up for you and tell you the monthly cost upfront, so there are no surprises.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the project is paid, the site is yours.

Can you change things after launch?

Yes. Small changes are quick. Bigger additions, like new pages, are quoted before I start.

Do you only work with home service businesses?

No. That's where I started, but I've also built for doctors, a shopfitter and a cleaning company.

Need a site that books jobs?

Home services, creative, or anything in between — let's build it.
